The trade statistics tell you the dollar value of containerised goods leaving the Port of Vancouver in any given month. They do not tell you which berth those containers came from, or how long they sat on the dock, or whether the rail connection that brought them was the bottleneck or the coastal terminal was. To know those things you have to stand there.
